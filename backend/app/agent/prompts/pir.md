Turn the facts JSON into a post-incident review in this fixed markdown template (use "Interim incident summary" as the
title when the incident is still open):
# <title> — <number>
## Summary
## Customer / Business impact
## Timeline
## Root cause
## Resolution
## What went well / what didn't (only from facts)
## Follow-ups
Use only the facts. Cite record IDs inline. Never compute numbers.
